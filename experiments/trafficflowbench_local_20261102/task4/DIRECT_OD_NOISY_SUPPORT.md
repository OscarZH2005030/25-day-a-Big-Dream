# OD measurement noise sensitivity

Both balanced policies are fitted with independent direct-measurement noise variance 0.25 in units of mean initial OD variance. Their reductions are compared on the same set of cells measured by neither policy. The policy that prioritizes other cells has smaller mean reduction under all five hypothetical variance laws, matching the direction of the exact-measurement comparison. The noise level is a sensitivity assumption.
