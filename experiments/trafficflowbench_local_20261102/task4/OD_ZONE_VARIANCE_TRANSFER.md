# Transferring origin-total selections across variance laws

Each selection rule fixes sixteen exact origin-total measurements under one variance law. Batch conditioning evaluates those locations under all three laws. Matching selection and evaluation reproduces sequential conditioning. Across ten roads, selecting under equal variance and evaluating under linear variance reduces the trace by 25.2%, compared with 31.3% when both use linear variance. Square-root selection loses at most 2.51 percentage points against the matched rule at either endpoint, in the road averages. Observations and variance laws are hypothetical.
