# Added common measurement bias

With locations and independent noise fixed, adding a shared error component with the same variance reduces the modeled gain in all 240 cases. The mean losses are 1.21 and 1.03 percentage points at the two noise levels. This experiment increases each observation's marginal noise variance and uses hypothetical observations.
