# Task4 source error amplitude at a common target budget

The 80 artificial cases compare the full feasible exchange with exactly one quarter of the same vector. At both amplitudes, all four sign combinations use a common realized target movement. The comparison rescales every fit to the smaller target movement across amplitudes before scoring.

For the agreement rule, independent lognormal noise changes from a positive aligned-minus-opposed mean at quarter amplitude to a negative mean at full amplitude. Independent positive background changes in the opposite direction. Both shared noise families retain negative contrasts. The synthetic targets are reused. These results measure sensitivity and do not establish a hidden OD improvement.
