# Negative correlation in zone-total measurement errors

This comparison fixes the selected origin totals and each measurement variance. With correlation -0.05, mean variance reduction exceeds the independent-noise case by 0.036 and 0.215 percentage points at the two noise levels. All 240 paired differences are positive on these ten roads. Together with the earlier positive-correlation experiment, this shows that the modeled gain need not increase monotonically with correlation. The observations and covariance assumptions are hypothetical.
